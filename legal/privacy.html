KSE Docs Tracker ("the Service") is operated by [Operator] on behalf of the Kyiv School of Economics ("KSE"). For any privacy question, contact [Contact e-mail].

We do not use analytics, advertising or tracking cookies. Google sign-in sets its own authentication cookie, which is needed for the Service to work.

3. How we use it

Only to provide, secure and maintain the Service. We do not sell data, build profiles, or use it for marketing.

4. Legal basis

Processing is necessary for KSE's legitimate interest in meeting its legal duty to publish public information on time, and for running KSE's internal systems securely. [To be confirmed under the Law of Ukraine "On Personal Data Protection" and, where it applies, the GDPR.]

5. Who else handles it

  • Google LLC / Google Cloud hosts the Service in the EU (Warsaw region) and provides sign-in, Drive and Sheets. Google acts as KSE's service provider under its own terms.
  • No other third parties receive data from the Service.

Google may process data outside Ukraine and the EU under its standard safeguards.

6. How long we keep it

  • The Service keeps no database of its own: document data stays in KSE's Drive and spreadsheets and is shown as it is there.
  • Google Cloud request logs are kept for 30 days by default, then deleted.
  • Status changes stay in «Звіт по документах» under KSE's normal record-keeping.
  • Data in your browser stays until you clear it.

7. Security

Access requires a KSE Google account. Changing data additionally requires an editor token. Traffic is encrypted (HTTPS), and the Service uses no stored passwords or key files.
