KSE Docs Tracker

An internal tool of the Kyiv School of Economics

KSE Docs Tracker shows KSE staff whether each document KSE must publish under Ukrainian education law is up to date.

  • Documents are grouped into nine blocks, following KSE's Google Drive folders.
  • Each document shows its relevance status and whether it is updated periodically, with its period and next deadline.
  • Each document name links to its folder in Google Drive.
  • Authorised staff can update a document's status; the change is written back to KSE's tracking spreadsheet.

Access is limited to KSE Google accounts. Signing in uses Google only to confirm that you are KSE staff.
